----------------------------------------------

Resolution:

- a weaker claimant with meaningful material/numeric compatibility or critical signal/timing/retry overlap is preserved.
- ambiguous ownership remains SHARED_REVIEW_REQUIRED.
Status: RESOLVED conservatively.

Status: RESOLVED conservatively.
