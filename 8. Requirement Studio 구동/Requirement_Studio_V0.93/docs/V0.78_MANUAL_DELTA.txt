Requirement Studio V0.78 Manual Delta
======================================

1. New automatic-evaluation handoff
-----------------------------------
`automatic_evaluation/guide_person/`
- guide_person_001.txt ...
- guide_person_questions.json
- manifest.json

GUIDE_PERSON asks reusable human Engineering Policy questions. It does not ask every SRS separately when one policy family can represent multiple examples.

Each question provides:
- stable GP-* ID
- policy topic and general question
- AI suggested position
- observed examples
- cross-document reuse scope
- YES / NO / CONDITIONAL / DEFER check choices
- free-form conditional rule / notes area

V0.78 does not automatically learn, store, or apply the human answer. Question quality is validated first; Human Policy Registry is a future version.
