Requirement Studio V0.72 -> V0.73 Change Decision
==================================================

Decision: IMPLEMENT / TRACEABILITY PRECISION HARDENING

Source of change
----------------
V0.72 Unified FINAL_RESULT identified two distinct ownership cases:
1. real exact-fragment gaps where Canonical critical facts exist in cited Source but selected fragments do not own them;
2. false-positive risk when one SRS legitimately owns an identifier and timing/retry facts across multiple exact fragments.

Implemented scope
-----------------
1. Critical-fact-aware Fragment Completion
   - Within already-matched Semantic Units / explicitly cited Source locations only.
   - Recover the minimum Source Fact Fragments that literally carry missing signal/timing/retry/enum facts.
   - Require strong identifier/timing/retry overlap or multiple critical-token overlap.
   - Never create Source facts, requirements, values, allocation, or ownership outside the matched Source range.
2. Multi-fragment exact ownership union
   - Exact ownership is evaluated over the union of all exact Source Fact Fragments linked to one SRS.
   - Audit exports token -> Source Fact Fragment ID evidence.
   - Broad structured roll-up cannot claim exact ownership for a value absent from the exact fragment literal.
3. Reviewer evidence precision
   - Unified 3-AI review is instructed not to re-flag an ownership gap when deterministic token->fragment evidence proves split-fragment ownership.
4. Existing OPEN Semantic Unit, SW Allocation Pending, Source Conflict, and Gold NOT_EVALUATED governance remains unchanged.
