-----
1. FINAL_RESULT self-identification
   - Version, Evaluation Mode, Source filename/SHA-256, Run ID, evaluated artifacts, Evidence SHA-256.

3. Critical Fact Fragment ownership gate
   - Broad Source provenance and exact Fragment ownership are audited separately.
   - Missing critical signal/timing/enum/retry ownership is TOOL_QUALITY / RELEASE_REVIEW.
4. Semantic terminal-disposition governance
   - REVIEW_NEEDED remains OPEN/non-terminal with explicit required-resolution text.
   - No Requirement, allocation, threshold, signal value, or owner is invented.
5. Existing conservative allocation / no-approved-Gold => NOT_EVALUATED policies remain unchanged.
