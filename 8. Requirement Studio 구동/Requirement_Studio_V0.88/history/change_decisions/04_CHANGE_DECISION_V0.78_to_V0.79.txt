Requirement Studio Change Decision — V0.78 -> V0.79
======================================================

Decision Summary
----------------
V0.79 introduces a persistent Human Policy Registry and the Review-First preservation rule requested after V0.78 GUIDE_PERSON validation.
The broader SYS/SWE E2E schema expansion is intentionally deferred to V0.80 so policy persistence / preservation / deterministic defect fixes can be verified separately.

Approved Human Policy Seed
--------------------------
- GP-ALLOC-001: YES
- GP-TC-001: YES
- GP-MIX-001: YES
- GP-OPEN-001: CONDITIONAL
  * OPEN Semantic Units are never auto-closed.
  * Human review must choose Canonical/SRS linkage, new/expanded SRS, Gap/Conflict, External Dependency, or justified Intentional Exclusion.
  * Unique normative behavior, condition logic, timing/numeric thresholds, prohibitions, error recovery, state transitions, or external-spec obligations must not be intentionally excluded.
- GP-SHARED-001: YES
- HP-PRESERVE-001: YES
  * REVIEW_REQUIRED/HOLD items are not deleted and remain visible in the Main Requirement Specification and Review View.

Implementation Decisions
------------------------
1) Persistent Human Policy Registry
   - External user store: %LOCALAPPDATA%\RequirementStudio\human_policy on Windows.
   - Fallback: REQUIREMENT_STUDIO_POLICY_HOME override, XDG_CONFIG_HOME, or ~/.requirement_studio/human_policy.
   - Files: human_policy_registry.json, human_policy_registry.txt, policy_change_log.jsonl.

2) Package Seed / Upgrade Safety
   - policy/human_policy_seed.json + .txt are included in the package.
   - Seed merges only missing Policy IDs.
   - Existing local human decisions and notes are never overwritten by a downloaded package.

3) GUIDE_PERSON Reuse
   - Candidate policy families are built as before.
   - YES/NO/CONDITIONAL decisions already present in the Registry are reused and removed from the new-question list.
   - DEFER remains an unresolved question.
   - Each Run writes guide_person/applied_policy_snapshot.json and .txt.

3-A) GUIDE_PERSON Human Decision Import
   - After a Run, an engineer may mark exactly one YES/NO/CONDITIONAL/DEFER checkbox in generated guide_person_###.txt.
   - The next Run imports explicit single-choice decisions into the external Registry before question resolution.
   - AI Suggested Position, blank selections and multi-selected answers are never treated as human approval.
   - Imported handoff SHA-256 and decision changes are recorded in the Registry ledger / append-only change log.

4) Review-First Main Requirement Preservation
   - V0.79 keeps the existing SWE.1 compatibility filename/view.
   - Main Requirement export now includes Eligible + Review Needed requirements.
   - Inferred/Pending requirements show `사람의 판단 필요 (REVIEW_REQUIRED[/HOLD])`.
   - Allocation/Review annexes remain; visibility in Main does not mean Official Release approval.
   - Full SYS/SWE naming/schema/view expansion is reserved for V0.80.

5) Deterministic defect fixes from V0.78
   - CHECK_SITUATION Deferred Intent snapshot counting excludes title/header/placeholder rows.
   - Exact-cited Semantic Unit/Fact Fragment provenance completion repairs Source-cited Eligible requirements without widening Source scope.
   - Fact-level allocation/domain deferral takes precedence over parent-wide SOURCE_INSUFFICIENT for Source-backed child facts such as JPEG-format clauses.

Safety Boundary
---------------
- Human policy is cross-document process guidance, not automatic per-SRS official allocation approval.
- Source-fidelity, unsupported-generation, exact-trace, conflict, visual-unknown, and arbitrary test-value safeguards remain active.
- Official Release remains independently gated.
