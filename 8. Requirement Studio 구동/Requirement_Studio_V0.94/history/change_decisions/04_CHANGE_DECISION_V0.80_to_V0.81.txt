Requirement Studio Change Decision — V0.80 → V0.81
===================================================

Trigger
-------
Actual V0.80 MLM and ESTRACK runs exposed two deterministic Tool Quality defects and one evaluation aggregation defect:
1) unrelated automatic-soldering Source ownership leaked into a brightness SRS and contaminated domain/allocation output;
2) an inferred SWE.6 Eligible SRS with a Source-backed `송출` behavior had zero generated TC because parent/fact allocation diverged;
3) GPT/Gemini/Claude equivalent zero-TC findings were split because Category labels differed.

Decision
--------
Create V0.81 as a narrow hardening release. Preserve V0.80 Engineering E2E, Human Policy Registry, OPEN governance, no-hallucination, and Review-First rules.

Approved Generic Changes
------------------------
- add shared Semantic Unit dominant-ownership validation above fragment-level containment;
- remove all derived trace/allocation structures of a proven unrelated claim;
- recompute allocation and E2E projection after ownership cleanup;
- recognize Korean `송출` as executable software-like Source behavior;
- forbid Parent SWE.6 Eligible + fact allocations + zero Eligible fact scope; explicitly defer instead;
- cluster cross-category reviewer findings only when concrete affected IDs and specific failure concepts match.

Rejected Approaches
-------------------
- no SRS_002/SRS_022/Paragraph 208 hardcoding;
- no C_PreCrankReq-specific exception;
- no forced SW allocation solely to increase TC count;
- no automatic closure of OPEN Semantic Units;
- no Gold registration before V0.81 actual-run verification and human review.

Verification
------------
- new V0.81 regression tests: 6 PASS
- total regression suite: 164/164 PASS
