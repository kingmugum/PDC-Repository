Requirement Studio V0.80 Manual Delta
======================================

V0.80 changes the center of the product from “select SWE.1/SWE.6 items” to “preserve Canonical Engineering Requirements and project them to the correct engineering/verification domains.”

New requirement views in the SWE.1-compatible Excel:
- 08_Engineering_Main: every Canonical Engineering Requirement
- 09_SYS1_System_View: System requirement candidates
- 10_SWE1_Software_View: Software requirement candidates
- 11_Human_Decision_View: REVIEW_REQUIRED/HOLD / human-decision items

New verification views in the SWE.6-compatible Excel:
- 6_SYS5_Candidates: Source-backed System Qualification candidates
- 7_Engineering_Verification: SYS.5/SWE.6 integrated trace

Important rules:
1) Not SWE.1 does not mean Not Requirement.
2) Not SWE.6 does not mean Not Verifiable.
3) REVIEW_REQUIRED/HOLD is preserved, not deleted.
4) SYS.5/pending facts cannot be used as qualifying SWE.6 assertions.
5) Candidate tests never fabricate missing inputs, criteria, results, PASS/FAIL or evidence.
6) Proposed/change-pending source interfaces remain explicitly review-required until resolved.

Human Policy Registry:
- Existing GP/HP decisions continue across versions.
- New core policies HP-E2E-001 and HP-SYSVER-001 are seeded without overwriting local human decisions.
